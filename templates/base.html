How to read this

What it does

Felixa Community Health reads how people in a community or team talk to each other and shows how healthy the conversation feels. You describe your workplace once, then run a new check whenever you have new messages.

What you see

Each message is scored from 0 to 3 for 13 tones, such as polite, encouraging, sarcastic or rude. Vibe Check adds an overall friendliness score out of 10, quotes the messages that need attention, and shows whether the mood is rising or falling over time. It also follows four measures through the conversation (escalation, conflict, empathy and passive-aggressive wording), lists alerts with a kind next step, and shows badges when the messages clearly show something good, such as courtesy or care.

Where your messages go

A model on this computer reads them and they stay in this app's database until you delete them. Only if you choose a Claude engine and tick the box do they go to Anthropic, and only to be read. Turn on Incognito first to take names out. When you delete the messages, the numbers of earlier checks (scores and counts, never messages, names or file names) are kept, so the next check can show what moved.

How much to trust it

A language model makes the judgement calls, and trained human raters have not yet checked them. Use the results to start a conversation, never to judge a person.
