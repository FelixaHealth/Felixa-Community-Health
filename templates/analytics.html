Felixa Community Health fetches each public page from the internet and reads the text the page contains. That is the only time it connects outside this computer. Comments that a page loads with scripts after it opens (YouTube, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, X) are not in that text, so they are not read. Comments written into the page itself, as on most forums and blogs, are read, but as plain lines of text with no names or times. Pages behind a login, such as Discord or WhatsApp, cannot be read from a link: export those and add the file.

Each check keeps the number of members it was made with, so the report can follow how the group grows or shrinks next to its health.
