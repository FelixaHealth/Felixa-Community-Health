How to read this
  • Blue flag. A message that is not heated and shows care, politeness or encouragement, right after heat.
  • Traction. The heat fell after it: no heated message followed in the same incident.
  • These describe wording in the chat, never a person. A calm message can also be a coincidence, so read the messages.
