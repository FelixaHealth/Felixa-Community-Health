Retrospective notes, spring season

The season started with a lot of energy. New members joined every week and the veterans took time to explain the maps, the callouts and the etiquette. People thanked each other for the help and the mood in voice chat stayed warm even when we lost.

Midway through the month we hit a rough patch. A few matches ended with sharp words and one player left a lobby in frustration. The conversation in the ranked channel turned cold for several days, and some newer members stopped posting there.

Two things helped. First, the officers started opening each session by naming one thing that went well in the last game. Second, we agreed on a short reset phrase that anyone can use when tension rises. Both habits were adopted quickly and people said they felt more comfortable asking questions again.

By the end of the month the tournament preparation was going well. Members volunteered to run practice sessions, wrote guides for beginners and organised a friendly scrim night. We are proud of how the group recovered and we want to build on it next season.

Next steps: keep the opening ritual, recognise the volunteers publicly, and invite the quieter members to share what they enjoy most about playing together.
